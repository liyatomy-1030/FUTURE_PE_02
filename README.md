# FUTURE_PE_02 - AI Content Marketing using UGC Ads

## Task

Create an AI-generated UGC ad content pack for a real or realistic product/business using structured prompts.

## Sample Brand

Brand name: SipLeaf  
Product: Herbal iced tea concentrate  
Category: D2C beverage  
Audience: College students, working professionals, fitness-conscious customers, and people looking for a low-sugar refreshment  
Platforms: Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels, YouTube Shorts  
Main goal: Increase product trials and website orders.

## Prompt Strategy

The prompt system focuses on short-form conversion content:

1. Define product, audience, pain point, and brand voice.
2. Generate scroll-stopping hooks.
3. Create UGC scripts using problem-solution-CTA structure.
4. Adapt scripts for Instagram, Facebook, and YouTube Shorts.
5. Generate captions and CTA variations.

## Structured Prompts Used

### Prompt 1 - Brand and Audience Mapping

```text
Act as a UGC ad strategist for short-form video campaigns.

Analyze this product:
- Brand: SipLeaf
- Product: Herbal iced tea concentrate
- Benefits: Low sugar, quick to prepare, refreshing, available in lemon mint and peach tulsi flavors
- Audience: Students, office workers, fitness-conscious customers, and people who want better alternatives to sugary drinks
- Brand voice: Fresh, honest, friendly, energetic
- Goal: Drive first-time orders

Return:
1. Main customer pain points
2. Product promise
3. Best emotional angle
4. Best rational angle
5. Content style recommendation
```

### Prompt 2 - Hook Generation

```text
Create 20 UGC ad hooks for SipLeaf.

Rules:
- Each hook must be short enough for the first 3 seconds of a reel.
- Use authentic, conversational language.
- Avoid exaggerated medical claims.
- Include a mix of problem hooks, curiosity hooks, comparison hooks, and lifestyle hooks.
```

### Prompt 3 - UGC Script Generation

```text
Write 5 UGC-style ad scripts for SipLeaf.

Each script should include:
- Hook
- Scene direction
- Problem
- Product introduction
- Benefit explanation
- Social proof or personal reaction
- CTA

Keep each script between 25 and 40 seconds.
Use a natural creator-style tone.
```

### Prompt 4 - Platform Adaptation

```text
Adapt the best SipLeaf UGC ad script for:
1. Instagram Reels
2. Facebook Reels
3. YouTube Shorts

For each platform, provide:
- Opening hook
- Script changes
- On-screen text
- Caption
- CTA
```

## UGC Ad Scripts

### Script 1 - Sugar Swap

Hook: I stopped buying sugary cold drinks for this.

Scene direction: Creator opens fridge, takes out SipLeaf, adds concentrate to a glass with ice and cold water.

Script: "I love having something cold in the evening, but I was tired of drinks that felt too sugary. So I tried SipLeaf, and now this is my go-to. You just add a little concentrate, cold water, ice, and it is ready. This one is lemon mint, and it tastes fresh without feeling heavy. If you want an easy drink at home that still feels like a treat, try SipLeaf."

CTA: Order your first bottle today.

### Script 2 - Office Break

Hook: Your 4 PM break needs this.

Scene direction: Creator sits at desk, looks tired, then prepares iced tea.

Script: "Around 4 PM, I always want something refreshing, but I do not want another coffee or soda. SipLeaf has been perfect for that. I keep it at home, mix it with cold water, and I have a proper iced tea in seconds. The peach tulsi flavor is light, fresh, and actually fun to drink during a work break."

CTA: Try SipLeaf for your next work break.

### Script 3 - Student Routine

Hook: Study drink upgrade in 20 seconds.

Scene direction: Creator shows books, laptop, and a quick drink setup.

Script: "When I study, I need something cold next to me, but I get bored of plain water. This SipLeaf concentrate makes iced tea so quickly. I just pour, mix, add ice, and I am done. It feels refreshing, tastes good, and saves me from ordering expensive drinks every time I sit down to study."

CTA: Make your study table better with SipLeaf.

### Script 4 - Hosting Friends

Hook: This is what I serve when friends come over.

Scene direction: Creator prepares a jug with ice, lemon slices, mint leaves, and SipLeaf.

Script: "If friends are coming over and I do not want to serve the same old soft drinks, I make this. SipLeaf turns into iced tea in seconds, and it looks so nice in a jug with ice and lemon slices. It is easy, refreshing, and everyone can pour their own glass."

CTA: Keep SipLeaf ready for your next hangout.

### Script 5 - Fitness Lifestyle

Hook: When you want something chilled but not too sweet.

Scene direction: Creator after a walk or workout, mixing drink in a bottle.

Script: "After a walk, I want something cold, but most drinks feel too sweet for me. SipLeaf is an easy option because I can control how strong I make it. I add a small amount, cold water, and ice. It is fresh, quick, and perfect when I want a lighter drink."

## Platform Adaptation

### Instagram Reels

Opening hook: I stopped buying sugary cold drinks for this.

Script changes: Keep the video fast, visually satisfying, and focused on the pour, ice, and first sip.

On-screen text:
- "Low-effort iced tea at home"
- "Add concentrate"
- "Cold water + ice"
- "Done in seconds"

Caption: My easiest evening drink swap. Fresh, quick, and not too sweet. Try SipLeaf if you love iced tea at home.

CTA: Tap to order.

### Facebook Reels

Opening hook: A refreshing iced tea you can make at home in seconds.

Script changes: Use a clearer explanation for family and working audiences. Show the bottle, preparation, and finished drink.

On-screen text:
- "Easy iced tea at home"
- "Great for work breaks"
- "Serve chilled"
- "Try lemon mint or peach tulsi"

Caption: Looking for a quick cold drink for home or work breaks? SipLeaf makes iced tea simple.

CTA: Order your first bottle.

### YouTube Shorts

Opening hook: Make cafe-style iced tea in under a minute.

Script changes: Use stronger pacing, tighter cuts, and a clear final product shot.

On-screen text:
- "20-second iced tea"
- "Pour"
- "Mix"
- "Sip"

Caption: Quick iced tea recipe with SipLeaf. Which flavor would you try first?

CTA: Subscribe for quick drink ideas and try SipLeaf.
